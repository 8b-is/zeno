設計思想と規格 (Philosophy & Japanese Roots)

1. 銭 (Zeni) — Value Earned, Not Borrowed

Unlike legacy fractional-reserve neo-banks that expand money supplies by writing debt loans with compound interest, Zeno anchors new liquidity exclusively in verified, proof-of-contribution utility gates (Foken).

2. 禅 (Zen) — Calm & Zero Anxiety

Financial anxiety is eliminated through spatial clarity (間 - Ma), transparent fees, and continuous micro-streaming (水流決済), settling balances by the second rather than monthly batch deadlines.

3. 善 (Sanpo Yoshi) — 三方よし

Edo merchant philosophy: Good for the payer, good for the payee, and good for society. Every settlement enriches the collective Universal Treasury.

4. 金継ぎ (Kintsugi) — The Golden Joinery Ledger

Transactions cannot be erased or hidden. Any adjustment or dispute resolution is joined with golden cryptographic witness signatures, honoring history rather than covering it up.
