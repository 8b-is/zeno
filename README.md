## 2. Japanese Roots & Core Pillars

### 2.1 銭 (Zeni) — Value Earned, Not Borrowed
In Japanese history, *Zeni* (銭) were tangible copper coins earned through craftsmanship and honest harvest. Zeno rejects debt-based money creation; balances are minted exclusively from verified fiat deposit or consensus-cleared Foken invoices.

### 2.2 禅 (Zen) — Meditative Stillness & Zero Anxiety
Zero dark patterns, zero overdraft penalties, zero spam notifications. The interface embraces **間 (Ma)**—intentional breathing room allowing serene, unclouded financial decision-making.

### 2.3 善 (Sanpo Yoshi) — 三方よし
The Edo merchant ethos of the Ōmi merchants:
- **売り手よし (Urite Yoshi)**: Fair reward for the contributor.
- **買い手よし (Kaite Yoshi)**: Zero rent-seeking tolls for the patron.
- **世間よし (Seken Yoshi)**: Every transaction enriches the public Universal Treasury.

### 2.4 金継ぎ (Kintsugi) — The Golden Joinery Ledger
Traditional banks hide reconciliations behind closed doors. In Zeno, the ledger is **strictly append-only and cryptographically chained via SHA-256**. All adjustments are sealed with golden witness receipts (`0x-kintsugi`).

### 2.5 水流決済 (Suiryū Kessai) — Continuous Micro-Streaming
Solving the Zeno paradox of motion: money streams continuously by the second ($\mu\text{JPY/sec}$), eliminating the archaic 30-day payroll lag.

### 4.5 Kintsugi Pack — zeno × wupz (金継ぎパック) & Zeroize Security Hardening
- **Incremental, content-addressed chain kompression** ([`spec/WUPZ_BRIDGE.md`](spec/WUPZ_BRIDGE.md), [`zeno_core/kompress.py`](apps/core/zeno_core/kompress.py)): fixed windows of sealed blocks, each addressed by SHA-256 and zlib-packed; on re-pack, full windows are reused verbatim and only the tail moves.
- **One seal decides**: if the previous head seal is still at the same height, everything below it is unchanged; if it moved, `HistoryRewriteError` — sealed history is refused, never re-packed.
- **Licence boundary**: wupz is AGPL-3.0-only; this is a clean-room, stdlib-only re-derivation of its ideas under ZENO's MIT licence. No wupz code is copied or linked.
- **Benchmarks**:
  - Synthetic 1000-block ledger: 557,282 → 131,411 bytes (4.24×); +1 block re-packs 1 of 63 windows.
  - [ICML 2026 45-Workshop OpenReview Corpus](spec/research/ICML_2026_WORKSHOPS_8B_IS.md): 53,537 → 15,024 bytes (**71.94% savings**, 6 content-addressed windows, head seal `2cc62240...57b8e`, `wupz` XOR fold `0xe766f0ccb8b0990e`).
- **Zeroize Security Remediation (`ZA-0006..ZA-0010`)**:
  - `ZA-0006`: Apple Wallet `pass_key.pem` encrypted at rest (PKCS#8 AES-256); `generate_signature()` fails closed on invalid keys/certs.
  - `ZA-0007`: `StripeBridge.webhook_secret` CPython heap buffer explicitly scrubbed on clear/teardown.
  - `ZA-0008`: `PaymentIntent.client_secret` minted from independent CSPRNG entropy (`secrets.token_hex`).
  - `ZA-0009`: `kompress.unpack()` scrubs raw decompressed frame locals before raising `PackIntegrityError`.
  - `ZA-0010`: CLI state enforced at `0600` (`~/.zeno/state.json`) and hardcoded `authenticationToken` redacted from `pass*.json` templates.

### 4.1 IC カード · Samsung Wallet 交通系カード (honest provisioning)

Suica / PASMO cannot be minted by any app: they are issued by JR East / the
PASMO Association into the **Mobile FeliCa (Type-F)** secure element, and
Android HCE (ISO-DEP A/B) cannot emulate Type F. `TransitCapability.probe()`
therefore reports the truth instead of a fake success:

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `android.hardware.nfc.felica` | Mobile FeliCa radio (JP SKUs only) |
| `com.felicanetworks.mfc` | Osaifu-Keitai client |
| `NFC + Samsung/Google Wallet` | **Contactless EMV** — tap Tokyo Metro / Toei gates with a bank card |

On devices without FeliCa the card shows `CONTACTLESS EMV READY` and routes to
Samsung Wallet (bank card → gate tap) plus the ZENO QR fallback; on JP devices
it routes to Mobile PASMO / Suica issuance, after which the card appears in
Samsung Wallet's 交通系IC slot.

A **physical** Suica / PASMO is read read-only over NFC-F (`PhysicalIcReader`):
balance + last 20 uses via FeliCa `READ_WO_ENCRYPTION` on system `0x0003`,
history service `0x090f`; no writes, IDm truncated, nothing leaves the device.
Full constraints, wire protocol and the commercial path:
[spec/TRANSIT_CARD_PROVISIONING.md](spec/TRANSIT_CARD_PROVISIONING.md).
