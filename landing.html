KINTSUGI (金継ぎ) · SANPO YOSHI (三方よし)

How every coin & fee stays honest

Japanese philosophy meets deterministic SHA-256 cryptography.

What does "Kintsugi SHA-256 Ledger" mean?

In Japan, Kintsugi repairs pottery with pure gold lacquer so every join is visible and stronger than before. In ZENO's ledger, every payment writes two matching lines — minus $25 from sender, plus $25 to receiver — so all lines always add up to zero.

Each receipt hashes itself onto the previous receipt (H(prev || tx)). Even changing one invisible thousandth of a cent breaks the golden chain immediately.

Where do subscription & trading fees go? (Sanpo Yoshi)

Kyoto merchants practiced Sanpo Yoshi — a deal must be good for the buyer, good for the seller, and good for society. Every fee on ZENO splits automatically in integer micro-dollars:

50% Operations (servers, Rust edge & security) · 30% Family Trust USDC (long-term Solana safety buffer) · 20% FOKEN Builders (engineers who genuinely save future work via the 0x62 net-utility gate).

NO JARGON · HONEST ANSWERS

Common questions

Which page should I open: /, /welcome, /us, or /us-rust?

/welcome (this page) is the calm illustrated guide and math sandbox. /us is the main everyday neo-bank app (Portfolio, $zenotag Pay, Swap, Solana QR, Cards, Stripe Pro). /us-rust runs 100% of the UI itself inside Rust WebAssembly via Leptos 0.8. And / is the original Tokyo Sovereign Neo-Bank constellation surface (Foken Gate, Apple/Google Wallet, FeliCa IC Card reader).

Is ZENO an FDIC-insured bank?

No — ZENO never misrepresents itself as an FDIC sponsor bank. Instead, ZENO uses 1:1 segregated USDC+USDT Proof-of-Reserve math in deterministic Rust, and routes fiat card/ACH subscriptions strictly through Stripe SAQ-A hosted checkout pages.

Do I need cryptocurrency before trying the app?

Not at all. Every screen works immediately offline or in browser sandbox mode with zero logins, and paid memberships support standard credit/debit cards & ACH via Stripe alongside optional direct Solana / Base USDC.

How do Android, Apple Wallet, and Tokyo Suica/PASMO fit in?

On iPhone/Mac, ZENO issues signed Apple Wallet passes (.pkpass) from the Constellation view. On Android, our native Kotlin + ARM64 Rust NEON APK (dev.vaked.zeno) supports read-only NFC-F physical Suica/PASMO inspection plus universal AEON Pay / Solana QR checkout.
